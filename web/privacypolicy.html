Kaizen privacy policy

Kaizen is a personal app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Health data

With your permission, Kaizen reads steps, active calories, sleep, weight, body fat and resting heart rate from Health Connect. It sends that data only to the Kaizen server running on your own computer, over your own network, where it is stored in a local database. It is never sold or shared.

AI coach

If you add a Claude API key, questions you ask the coach and a summary of your recent data are sent to Anthropic to generate answers. Food photos you choose to analyse are sent the same way.

Removing data

You can revoke Health Connect access at any time in Health Connect settings, and delete the data folder on your computer to remove everything.
