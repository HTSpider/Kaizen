Install Kaizen

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app. Chrome may show a blank grey page. That's normal: the file downloads in the background (about 38 MB).
  2. If Chrome asks "Download anyway?" or warns the file may be harmful, tap Download. The warning appears for any app that isn't from the Play Store.
  3. When it finishes, tap Open in the bar at the bottom. If you missed it, pull down your notifications and tap kaizen.apk, or open Chrome's ⋮ menu → Downloads.
  4. Allow installs from Chrome if Android asks, then tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Kaizen from your app drawer and enter:
    address
    and the pair code shown in Kaizen on your PC under Settings → Phone.

After this one install, Kaizen updates itself whenever you open it on your home Wi-Fi.
